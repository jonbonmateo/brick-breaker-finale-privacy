Brick Breaker Finale

Privacy Policy · Last updated July 26, 2026

Summary: Brick Breaker Finale collects nothing. There are no user accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no analytics. Everything the game remembers is stored only on your own device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Who we are

Brick Breaker Finale ("the app") is a single-player arcade game published by jonbonmateo ("the Developer") for the Google Play Store. This policy explains what information is, and isn't, handled when you play.

Information the app collects

The app collects no personal information whatsoever. There are no user accounts and no login. It does not ask you for your name, email, photos, contacts, or any other personal information, and it contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Your score, coins, level progress, unlocked themes and skins, achievements, daily-challenge streak, and sound preference are stored only on your own device using local app storage. This information never leaves your device, is never transmitted to the Developer or to anyone else, and is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app contains no advertising of any kind. There is no advertising SDK in the app, no ads are displayed, and no advertising identifier, location data, or usage data is collected or shared for advertising purposes.

Purchases

The app contains no in-app purchases. Every theme, brick skin, and paddle skin in the shop is unlocked with coins earned by playing the game. No billing library is present, no payment is ever processed, and no financial information is collected.

Leaderboards & achievements

The per-level best-score tracker and the achievements list are for entertainment only. They compare your own locally-stored results against your own past results. There is no shared or online leaderboard, nothing is transmitted to a server, and you are never identified to other players.

Permissions

The app requests only two permissions:

  • Vibration — used for haptic feedback during gameplay.
  • Internet — declared by the app framework the game is built on. The game itself is fully offline: it bundles all of its content in the app and does not send or receive any data over the network.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, or files.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information at all, there is nothing for a parent or guardian to request access to or deletion of.

Data sharing & selling

The Developer does not sell or share your data, because the app does not collect any personal data to begin with.

Data deletion

All data the app stores is local to your device. Uninstalling the app permanently removes it. There is no server-side account or record to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page with a new "Last updated" date. If a future update ever introduces advertising, purchases, or any form of data collection, this policy and the app's Google Play Data Safety section will both be updated before that version is released.

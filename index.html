Brick Breaker Finale

Privacy Policy · Last updated August 13, 2026

Summary: Brick Breaker Finale has no user accounts and no analytics, and the Developer collects nothing about you directly. The app shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob and offers optional real-money purchases through Google Play Billing — both are handled entirely by Google, and what each one collects is described below under Advertising and Purchases. Every purchase is optional: everything it unlocks can also be earned for free by playing. Everything else the game remembers is stored only on your own device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Who we are

Brick Breaker Finale ("the app") is a single-player arcade game published by jonbonmateo ("the Developer") for the Google Play Store. This policy explains what information is, and isn't, handled when you play.

Information the app collects

The app collects no personal information whatsoever on its own. There are no user accounts and no login. It does not ask you for your name, email, photos, contacts, or any other personal information, and it contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The two third-party SDKs in the app are Google AdMob (advertising) and Google Play Billing (purchases), described in their own sections below.

Your score, coins, level progress, unlocked themes and skins, achievements, daily-challenge streak, and sound preference are stored only on your own device using local app storage. This information never leaves your device, is never transmitted to the Developer or to anyone else, and is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google. To serve, cap and measure those ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier)
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • ad interaction data — that an ad was shown, viewed or tapped

This information is collected by Google, not by the Developer. It is handled under Google's own privacy policy, and the Developer receives only anonymous, aggregated earnings reports that cannot identify individual players. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Personalised ads and your choices

Ads may be personalised based on the information above, depending on your consent and your device settings. You are in control:

  • On your device: open Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by manufacturer) to delete or reset your advertising ID, or to opt out of ads personalisation. Ads will still appear, but they will not be personalised.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change or withdraw that choice at any time.
  • Where required by law or by Google Play's Families policy, only non-personalised ads are requested.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The shop offers optional permanent upgrades (things like extra balls, longer power-ups, or a starting-combo bonus). Every one of them can also be unlocked for free with coins earned by playing — a real-money purchase only skips that grind, priced at roughly $1 each. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing; the app never sees or stores your payment details (card number, billing address, etc.) — that information goes directly to Google.

To process a purchase and let you restore it later (for example after reinstalling the app or switching devices), Google Play Billing shares with the app:

  • which product you bought, and a purchase token identifying that transaction
  • whether the purchase is still valid (owned) or has been refunded

The app stores that ownership status locally on your device so your upgrades stay unlocked. No purchase or payment data is sent to the Developer or to any server the Developer controls — there is no receipt-validation server in this app. Google's own privacy policy governs the data Google Play Billing itself collects to process the transaction; see the Google Privacy Policy.

A Restore Purchases button in the shop lets you re-sync ownership of past purchases with Google Play at any time.

Leaderboards & achievements

The per-level best-score tracker and the achievements list are for entertainment only. They compare your own locally-stored results against your own past results. There is no shared or online leaderboard, nothing is transmitted to a server, and you are never identified to other players.

Permissions

The app requests these permissions:

  • Internet and Network state — used to request advertisements and to check whether a connection is available. The game itself bundles all of its content and is playable offline; only the ads need a connection.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) — lets the ad library read the resettable advertising ID described above.
  • Vibration — used for haptic feedback during gameplay.
  • Billing — lets Google Play Billing process purchases made in the shop, described under Purchases above.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, storage, or files.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences. The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Where the app is distributed to a child audience, or where a user is identified as a child, ad requests are marked so that only non-personalised ads are served, in line with COPPA and Google Play's Families policy. Real-money purchases are processed by Google Play Billing under your device's own Google account; parents can restrict or require approval for purchases using the parental control and purchase-approval settings built into the Google Play Store app.

Data sharing & selling

The Developer does not sell your data and does not collect personal data to begin with. Data handled by the Google AdMob SDK for advertising, and by Google Play Billing for purchases, is governed by Google's own privacy policy, linked above. Neither SDK's data passes through a server the Developer operates.

Data deletion

All data the app itself stores is local to your device. Uninstalling the app permanently removes it. There is no server-side account or record to delete. Purchase records are kept by Google Play under your own Google account, independent of the app; manage or request deletion of that data through your Google account settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page with a new "Last updated" date. If a future update changes how advertising or purchases work, or introduces any further data collection, this policy and the app's Google Play Data Safety section will both be updated before that version is released.

Brick Breaker Finale

Privacy Policy · Last updated July 28, 2026

Summary: Brick Breaker Finale has no user accounts, no in-app purchases, and no analytics, and the Developer collects nothing about you. The app does show advertisements supplied by Google AdMob, and Google collects device and advertising information in order to serve and measure those ads — see Advertising below. Everything else the game remembers is stored only on your own device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Who we are

Brick Breaker Finale ("the app") is a single-player arcade game published by jonbonmateo ("the Developer") for the Google Play Store. This policy explains what information is, and isn't, handled when you play.

Information the app collects

The app collects no personal information whatsoever. There are no user accounts and no login. It does not ask you for your name, email, photos, contacts, or any other personal information, and it contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The only third-party SDK in the app is the Google AdMob advertising library described under Advertising below.

Your score, coins, level progress, unlocked themes and skins, achievements, daily-challenge streak, and sound preference are stored only on your own device using local app storage. This information never leaves your device, is never transmitted to the Developer or to anyone else, and is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google. To serve, cap and measure those ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier)
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • ad interaction data — that an ad was shown, viewed or tapped

This information is collected by Google, not by the Developer. It is handled under Google's own privacy policy, and the Developer receives only anonymous, aggregated earnings reports that cannot identify individual players. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Personalised ads and your choices

Ads may be personalised based on the information above, depending on your consent and your device settings. You are in control:

  • On your device: open Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by manufacturer) to delete or reset your advertising ID, or to opt out of ads personalisation. Ads will still appear, but they will not be personalised.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change or withdraw that choice at any time.
  • Where required by law or by Google Play's Families policy, only non-personalised ads are requested.

Purchases

The app contains no in-app purchases. Every theme, brick skin, and paddle skin in the shop is unlocked with coins earned by playing the game. No billing library is present, no payment is ever processed, and no financial information is collected.

Leaderboards & achievements

The per-level best-score tracker and the achievements list are for entertainment only. They compare your own locally-stored results against your own past results. There is no shared or online leaderboard, nothing is transmitted to a server, and you are never identified to other players.

Permissions

The app requests these permissions:

  • Internet and Network state — used to request advertisements and to check whether a connection is available. The game itself bundles all of its content and is playable offline; only the ads need a connection.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) — lets the ad library read the resettable advertising ID described above.
  • Vibration — used for haptic feedback during gameplay.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, storage, or files.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences. The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Where the app is distributed to a child audience, or where a user is identified as a child, ad requests are marked so that only non-personalised ads are served, in line with COPPA and Google Play's Families policy.

Data sharing & selling

The Developer does not sell your data and does not collect personal data to begin with. Data handled by the Google AdMob SDK for advertising purposes is governed by Google's privacy policy, linked above.

Data deletion

All data the app stores is local to your device. Uninstalling the app permanently removes it. There is no server-side account or record to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page with a new "Last updated" date. If a future update changes how advertising works, or introduces purchases or any further data collection, this policy and the app's Google Play Data Safety section will both be updated before that version is released.
